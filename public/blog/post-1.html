A Closer Look at Rainbow Rope Sizes and Colours

  1. Rainbow Ropes Team
  2. 15 Sep, 2026

Start with the size, choose the packing, and share the quantity you need. These three details make a product enquiry much clearer.

Start with the diameter

The size shown in millimetres refers to the rope's diameter. In the product collection, you will find 2 mm, 3 mm, 4 mm, 5 mm and 6 mm rope pages. Each includes a photograph and a summary of the product's construction and packing.

If you are matching rope you already buy, start with the size listed on its packaging or your previous order. If you are looking for a new requirement, describe what you plan to use it for when you contact us. A photograph gives you a useful view of the product, while the written specification makes the size clear.

Look at the colours shown

The collection photographs show red, green, blue, orange, yellow and fluorescent yellow ropes. These bright colours make individual bundles easy to distinguish when arranging a display, separating stock or referring to a particular item.

When a colour matters to your order, name it in your enquiry and ask the team to confirm availability. The colour swatches on each page are a guide to the colours pictured; the product photograph gives you a closer view of the rope itself.

Check how the rope is packed

Diameter and packing are separate choices. For example, the 2 mm range includes individually wrapped bundles, coils and a packet format. The 4 mm collection includes tied bundles and a Rainbow-branded packet. Open the matching product page to see the specific format rather than choosing by size alone.

  • Bundles: folded rope secured into a manageable shape.
  • Coils: rope wound into compact loops.
  • Packets: several rope bundles presented together in a pack.

Bring the details together

Once you have found a product, note its name, diameter and packing format. Add your preferred colours, required length and quantity, then contact Ravi Enterprise to discuss the order. The team can confirm the details available for your requirement and provide a quotation.

Ready to compare the options? Explore the Rainbow product collection, or read our guide to coils, bundles and packets for a closer look at packing.
