Coils, Bundles or Packets: Choosing Your Rope Packing

  1. Rainbow Ropes Team
  2. 15 Sep, 2026

When you compare ropes, the diameter is often the first detail you notice. Packing deserves a closer look too. The same size may be presented in different formats, and knowing which one you need helps you describe your order clearly.

The Rainbow collection includes coils, tied bundles and packet formats. Each product page shows the packing in its photograph, so you can compare the arrangement before discussing quantities with Ravi Enterprise.

A packing format describes how the rope is presented. Always confirm the length and quantity separately when requesting a quote.

Coils: neatly wound rope

A coil is rope wound into a series of loops. In the Rainbow 2 mm Rope Coils photograph, the colourful coils are stacked together inside a clear bag. The shape gives you an immediate visual distinction from the longer folded bundles elsewhere in the range.

If you prefer this format, include the word “coils” in your enquiry along with the diameter. Ask about the length per coil and the number of coils in the quoted pack, so you can compare the quotation with your stock requirement.

Bundles: folded and tied

A bundle is rope folded into an elongated shape and secured with ties. The 3 mm, 4 mm, 5 mm and 6 mm product photographs show this arrangement in assorted colours. The 2 mm bundles are individually wrapped.

Check the photograph and packing description together. A tied bundle and an individually wrapped bundle may have a similar shape, but they are presented differently. Specifying the exact product name makes it easier for the team to understand which one you mean.

Packets: bundles grouped together

A packet brings several rope bundles together in one outer pack. Rainbow's 2 mm Rope Packet and 4 mm Rope Packet show assorted colours in branded clear packaging. The 4 mm packet photograph also shows individual product labels on its bundles.

For a packet enquiry, ask the team to confirm the contents of the quoted pack. The number of bundles, length per bundle and colour assortment are useful details to have before planning how many packets to order.

Compare like for like

A clear packing description helps whether you are buying for your own requirements or arranging stock for your shop. Before confirming an order, make sure you understand:

  • The rope diameter and exact product format.
  • The length supplied per bundle or coil.
  • The number of units in a packet or outer pack.
  • The colours included and the quantity being quoted.

You can browse all packing formats in the product collection or share your requirements with Ravi Enterprise. For a simple checklist of what to send, read our bulk rope enquiry guide.
